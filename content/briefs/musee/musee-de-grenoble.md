## Primary task

Explain why the museum’s historic commitment to modern art makes it distinctive, while making current free permanent access and the Egyptian-department closure operationally clear.

## Visit proposition

Choose Grenoble when you want old masters plus one of France’s earliest institutional commitments to modern art in the same museum, with current permanent collections free to visit.

## MUST

- Andry-Farcy / early modern-art acquisition story;
- broad old + modern + contemporary scope;
- current permanent collections free;
- current hours / Tuesday closure;
- current Egypt department temporary closure;
- no unsupported guarantee of specific displayed works.

## SHOULD

- explain Agutte-Sembat role where it clarifies collection development;
- keep first-Sunday policy current and sourced;
- visitor services only where useful.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer + quick facts.
2. Why Grenoble became an early modern-art museum.
3. Collection continuity from old masters to modern/contemporary, written as a historical progression rather than three cards.
4. Current free-access information as a compact practical block.
5. Separate high-visibility notice for Egypt closure.
6. Grenoble/modern/free-entry next links.
