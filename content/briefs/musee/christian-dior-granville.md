## Primary task

Help users understand the museum as a seasonal annual-exhibition venue in Dior’s childhood villa, then make booking/accessibility constraints impossible to miss.

## Visit proposition

Choose the Christian Dior Museum for the year’s exhibition interpreted through Villa Les Rhumbs and its garden — not for a fixed permanent house-museum display.

## MUST

- annual exhibition model;
- current 2026 exhibition and season dates;
- current monthly hours / last admission;
- current tariffs/free days;
- online booking strongly recommended;
- villa inaccessible to PMR and pushchairs because three levels are stair-only;
- garden separate, free and year-round.

## SHOULD

- explain the villa’s childhood context without implying room-by-room domestic reconstruction;
- keep garden and villa operationally distinct;
- date all exhibition/season statements.

## Structure for this page

1. 2026 exhibition and season first.
2. Quick practical facts.
3. Why the annual exhibition is the core visit.
4. **Two-place context**: villa + garden, each with its own role and access rules; no three-card reasons grid.
5. High-visibility accessibility constraint.
6. Current booking/hours/tariffs.
7. Mode/Normandy/exhibitions next links.

SEMI_STABLE: accessibility and garden schedule.
STABLE: childhood-villa relation and annual-exhibition model.

## Anti-patterns

- describing the 2026 exhibition as permanent;
- generic three-reason section;
- hiding stair-only access after ticket information;
- claiming the villa is a preserved domestic interior throughout;
- carrying current season copy past 1 November without recheck.

## Success criteria

The visitor understands exactly what is on in 2026, that the museum is seasonal, and whether the villa is physically accessible to their party before booking.
