## Primary task

Prevent the common planning mistake of treating Musée Granet and Granet XXe as one undifferentiated place/ticket. Help the visitor choose the site that matches their interest.

## Visit proposition

Choose first between the main museum (archaeology, old masters, Granet/Ingres/Cézanne context) and Granet XXe (Jean Planque and twentieth-century art). They are complementary but operationally distinct.

## MUST

- two separate sites/addresses;
- current separate-ticket logic;
- main-site collection identity;
- Granet XXe / Jean Planque identity;
- current seasonal hours, including November 2026 change;
- no unsupported guarantee of specific current works.

## SHOULD

- explain Cézanne without turning the museum into a monographic Cézanne site;
- surface PMR entrance if current/useful;
- make site choice visually obvious before chronology/artists.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer: “two sites, choose first”.
2. Quick facts.
3. **Two-site comparison** with one substantial block per site: address, collection logic, ticket consequence.
4. What connects the two sites historically/artistically.
5. Seasonal 2026 hours and practical rules.
6. Regional/beaux-arts/preparation links.

Do not fall back to three period cards after explaining a two-site decision.

## Freshness

VOLATILE: hours by season, prices, exhibitions, site closures.
SEMI_STABLE: accessibility entrances/display routing.
STABLE: two-site structure and collection identities.

Before reading artist names, the visitor knows which Granet site they actually need and that tickets/hours may differ.
