## Primary task

Show why the place of creation is inseparable from the sculptures, then surface the current entrance/work constraint and free permanent access.

## Visit proposition

Choose Bourdelle when you want to understand sculpture through the artist’s actual working environment: historic studios, apartment, plaster hall, later extensions and gardens.

## MUST

- historic studio authenticity / long working history;
- sculpture process/material context without fake personal experience;
- free permanent collections;
- current hours;
- **temporary access via 16 rue Antoine-Bourdelle during reception works**;
- current accessibility information.

## SHOULD

- structure content through actual spaces rather than “three reasons”;
- include gardens and hall des plâtres as visit components;
- distinguish temporary exhibition conditions from permanent-free access.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer + quick facts.
2. Why the studio changes the way the work is understood.
3. **Move through the place**: atelier → apartment → hall des plâtres → gardens/extensions. Flexible spatial sequence, not fixed card count.
4. Current 2026 entrance/work notice.
5. Free permanent visit + practical information.
