## Primary task

Help a visitor decide whether the Fondation is worth planning **for the actual date considered**, because opening status and exhibition programming fundamentally change the visit.

## Visit proposition

Choose the Fondation when a current exhibition interests you and/or when Frank Gehry’s architecture is itself part of the desired cultural visit. Date/status comes before generic description.

## Ownership and exclusions

Own the institution, architecture, current opening status, access and stable programme-led visit logic.

Exclude detailed exhibition interpretation that should belong to `/expositions/` or a future exhibition-specific page.

## MUST

- current closure and announced reopening 9 October 2026;
- explain programme-led visit logic;
- Frank Gehry building as a genuine second visit motive;
- current tariffs / booking / access;
- avoid implying a stable permanent-gallery route always exists;
- checked date next to volatile status.

## SHOULD

- direct visitors to verify the exhibition and calendar together;
- mention official architecture tools/app where useful;
- explain why the same address can produce a different visit from one season to another.

## OPTIONAL

- commissioned works only where current and sourced;
- family/accessibility details only if decision-relevant.

## Structure for this page

1. **Current status notice first** while closed.
2. What kind of institution/visit this is.
3. Two complementary reasons for choosing it: current programme + Gehry architecture — use a two-part editorial layout, not three cards.
4. Current practical booking/access information.
5. Links to Paris alternatives and exhibition hub.

## Facts / freshness

VOLATILE: open/closed, reopening, exhibition, hours, prices, navette, booking.
STABLE: Gehry authorship and institutional location.

Rechecked 2026-09-14 on official Fondation pages.

## Entities

Fondation Louis Vuitton; Frank Gehry; Bois de Boulogne; current exhibition only as a dated temporary entity.

## Internal links

- `/musees/paris/` for alternatives when closed;
- `/themes/musees-art-moderne-contemporain/` for institution comparison;
- `/expositions/paris/` for current programme comparison.

## Anti-patterns

- evergreen wording that hides a current closure;
- architectural superlatives without evidence;
- three generic reasons to visit;
- exhibition detail that cannibalizes the exhibition cluster;
- invented crowd/queue advice.

## Success criteria

A visitor immediately knows whether the Fondation is open on the relevant period, what fundamentally determines the visit, and why architecture can remain relevant beyond a specific exhibition.
