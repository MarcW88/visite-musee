## Primary task

Explain the museum’s interdisciplinary logic and help a visitor understand the four permanent questions/spaces before giving current visit constraints.

## Visit proposition

Choose Confluences when you want natural sciences, anthropology and social/cultural questions deliberately mixed around four big themes rather than separated into disciplinary departments.

## MUST

- preserve the four permanent spaces/questions: Origines, Espèces, Sociétés, Éternités;
- explain why cross-disciplinary display matters;
- current 2026 closure/reworking of `Sociétés` from 22 September;
- current hours, ticket conditions and access;
- architecture/location at Rhône-Saône confluence;
- no generic “science museum” reduction.

## SHOULD

- surface distinctive sensory/comfort tools when useful;
- explain free-access architectural/public spaces separately from ticketed galleries;
- temporary programme only as a supplement, not page owner.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer: “large questions, not disciplines”.
2. Quick facts.
3. **Four permanent questions** — four distinct blocks/cards or a 2×2 grid because four is intrinsic here.
4. Current 2026 route notice for `Sociétés`.
5. Architecture/confluence context.
6. Practical visit + useful comfort/access tools.
7. Lyon/science/family next links.

Do not compress four permanent spaces into three generic cards.

## Freshness

VOLATILE: `Sociétés` closure/reopening, hours, tariffs, exhibitions, transport disruptions.
SEMI_STABLE: family/accessibility/sensory tools.
STABLE: broad curatorial model and architectural/location identity.

## Anti-patterns

- three-card template;
- catalogue of random specimens;
- calling it only a natural-history museum;
- undated statement that all four spaces are open during the 2026 works period;
- fake duration/crowd advice.

## Success criteria

The user understands the four-question model, knows the current route limitation, and can decide whether an interdisciplinary museum is what they want.
