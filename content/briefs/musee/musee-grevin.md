## Primary task

Help users understand what kind of outing Grévin actually is and whether that staged/pop-cultural format suits them better than a conventional art/history museum, then make ticket/calendar variability clear.

## Visit proposition

Choose Grévin for wax figures, staged scenes, photography/interactivity and the heritage of a historic entertainment venue — not for a collection of original artworks.

## MUST

- explain the experience format early;
- current >250-personality claim only with official source;
- Palais des Mirages / historic décor as real heritage component;
- dynamic dated-price model and current undated price;
- hours vary by date;
- under-5/current age rules where stated;
- no fake museum-review score or simulated visit experience.

## SHOULD

- explain who will likely prefer another museum;
- keep celebrity examples sparse because roster is volatile;
- distinguish historic elements from current campaign zones.

## OPTIONAL

- current campaign zone only if it materially changes the visit and is dated;
- family offer only if current and sourced.

## Structure for this page

1. Fast decision answer: museum-as-staged-experience.
2. Practical facts.
3. What the visit consists of: figures + sets + historic illusion/spectacle.
4. A **chronology / experience sequence** for 1882 → Palais des Mirages → evolving contemporary gallery, rather than a generic three-reason grid.
5. Date-dependent tickets and hours.
6. Alternatives by family / unusual museum / Paris comparison.

## Freshness

VOLATILE: prices, special offers, hours, personalities, zones.
STABLE: broad wax/scenography identity and Palais des Mirages heritage.

## Entities

Musée Grévin; Palais des Mirages; wax-figure/staged experience; current campaign entities only when dated.

## Anti-patterns

- art-museum vocabulary implying original artworks;
- stale list of celebrities;
- fixed single “ticket price” when pricing is dynamic;
- generic three-card “reasons to visit”;
- invented queue/crowd observations.

## Success criteria

The visitor can decide quickly whether the entertainment-led format matches the outing they want and understands that price/hours must be checked for the chosen date.
