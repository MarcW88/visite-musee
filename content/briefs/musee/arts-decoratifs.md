## Primary task

Help visitors decide which object/design lens they want to follow in an institution whose breadth is much larger than a single “decorative arts” category, while warning that some fragile collections rotate.

## Visit proposition

Choose the Musée des Arts Décoratifs when you want to understand aesthetic and social change through objects and designed environments — furniture, interiors, design, fashion, jewellery, glass, toys and graphic design — rather than through painting alone.

## MUST

- object/design-led proposition;
- broad chronology from Middle Ages to today;
- current collection families from official portal;
- explicit caveat that some fragile holdings are not permanently displayed;
- current full price / under-26 free condition;
- current hours/nocturne nuance only when verified;
- separate identity from Musée Nissim de Camondo.

## SHOULD

- allow visitors to choose a collection lens rather than present all categories as equally central;
- explain why use/function matters alongside aesthetics;
- mention ticket bundling only if it helps planning.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer + practical facts.
2. Why objects tell a different history from paintings.
3. **Choose a collection lens** — flexible set of 4–6 lenses (historic interiors/furniture; modern design; fashion/textile; jewellery; graphics/photo/wallpaper; toys/other object cultures), not three cards.
4. What may rotate and why that matters before visiting.
5. Practical visit / ticket.
6. Theme/Paris/exhibition next links.

## Freshness

VOLATILE: prices, exhibitions, nocturne conditions, current rotations.
SEMI_STABLE: ticket bundling, visitor services.
STABLE: broad collection identity and media families.

## Entities

Musée des Arts Décoratifs / MAD Paris; official collection families; Musée Nissim de Camondo as related but separate entity.

## Anti-patterns

- three generic period cards;
- implying fashion/textile/graphics are always visible;
- conflating MAD with Camondo;
- object-name stuffing from the online database;
- invented recommended duration.

## Success criteria

The visitor can decide which collection family motivates the trip and understands that visible holdings depend partly on rotations/exhibitions.
