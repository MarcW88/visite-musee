# Content brief — Musée du Louvre

workflow_version: museum-v1
status: BRIEF_READY

## Visit proposition
Choose the Louvre when the goal is to cross several civilizations and artistic periods in one palace. The visitor's central problem is selection, not lack of things to see.

## Page task
Answer two questions early: why choose the Louvre, and how should a first visit be narrowed into a coherent route?

## MUST
- Specific proposition, not fame-based praise.
- Eight-department breadth expressed without encyclopedic dumping.
- Current 2026 hours / prices / booking guidance.
- Official room-opening calendar as a visit constraint.
- No invented recommended duration.

## Structure derived from research
1. Direct decision answer.
2. Selection problem: which Louvre inside the Louvre?
3. Three useful route logics.
4. Palace as part of the experience.
5. 2026 practical planning.

## Anti-patterns
No “10 masterpieces you must see”, no crowd claim without methodology, no claim that a collection work is currently displayed, no generic three-reasons template copied from other museum pages.
