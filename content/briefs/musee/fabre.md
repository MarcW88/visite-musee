## Primary task

Help visitors understand the museum through the relationships that give its broad collection a local and historical identity, rather than reducing it to “European art from the Renaissance to today”.

## Visit proposition

Choose Fabre for a broad fine-arts route whose nineteenth-century identity is shaped by Alfred Bruyas and Courbet/Delacroix, then extended to modern/contemporary painting through Montpellier-linked artists and the Soulages donation.

## MUST

- broad old-master → contemporary scope;
- Bruyas as a central collector/donor thread;
- Courbet/Delacroix context without turning the page into an artist list;
- Soulages donation as a meaningful endpoint;
- current hours/prices/free-first-Sunday information;
- preserve collection vs current-display caution.

## SHOULD

- mention Hôtel Sabatier as the arts-decorative department when current access/ticket relationship is clear;
- use donor/collection relationships as the narrative spine;
- explain why Montpellier matters to the collection.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer + quick facts.
2. Bruyas: why a collector changes the nineteenth-century story.
3. **A collection built by relationships** — old masters → Bruyas/Courbet → Montpellier artists → Soulages, as a narrative sequence rather than three equal cards.
4. Hôtel Sabatier as a separate optional branch when verified.
5. Practical visit.
6. Montpellier/beaux-arts/free-entry links.
