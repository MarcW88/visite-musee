## Primary task

Explain why Louvre-Lens is not a smaller copy of the Louvre in Paris: the visit is built around an open chronological Galerie du Temps, plus architecture and park, with free permanent access.

## Visit proposition

Choose Louvre-Lens when you want to read roughly 5,000 years in one open chronological gallery and prefer a simpler permanent route to the departmental organisation of the Louvre in Paris.

## MUST

- explain Galerie du Temps concept;
- current >250 works / renewed presentation context with official source;
- free permanent access;
- temporary-gallery ticket distinction;
- current hours / Tuesday closure;
- architecture/mining-site context;
- no implication that it duplicates Paris collections.

## SHOULD

- explain why chronology changes interpretation;
- make current transport/free-TADAO information date-sensitive;
- use Louvre Paris comparison as curatorial contrast, not SEO keyword repetition.

## Success criteria
