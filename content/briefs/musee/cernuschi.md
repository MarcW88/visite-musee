## Primary task

Help visitors understand the collection focus and scale of Cernuschi, then use official route options and free permanent access to make the visit easy to plan.

## Visit proposition

Choose Cernuschi for a focused introduction to East Asian art — especially Chinese and Japanese collections, complemented by Korean and Vietnamese holdings — in a permanent route that is currently free.

## MUST

- keep China, Japan, Korea and Vietnam legible as four distinct collection areas;
- permanent collection free / temporary exhibition ticket distinction;
- current hours and reservation logic;
- no claim that every collection object is currently displayed;
- use official 45-minute and ~1h30 app routes only as sourced route options, not editorial guesses.

## SHOULD

- explain why the museum is more manageable/focused than a large encyclopedic institution;
- connect Henri Cernuschi only where his collecting history helps explain the holdings;
- make the official route choices a practical aid.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer + quick facts.
2. Why this focused scale matters.
3. **Four collection lenses**: China / Japan / Korea / Vietnam. Four blocks, not three.
4. **Choose your official route**: highlights ~45 min vs fuller ~1h30 app route, clearly attributed to the museum.
5. Permanent-free vs temporary-paid visit conditions.
6. Paris/free-entry next links.

## Freshness

VOLATILE: exhibitions, temporary prices, opening exceptions, reservation rules.
SEMI_STABLE: app routes and visitor tools.
STABLE: broad collection geography/history.

## Entities

Musée Cernuschi; Henri Cernuschi where useful; Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese collections; official visit app/routes.

## Internal links

- `/musees/paris/`
- `/visiter/musees-gratuits/`
- relevant theme pages only when semantically accurate.

## Anti-patterns

- collapsing Korea + Vietnam just to fit three cards;
- calling the museum a summary of “all Asian art”;
- invented ideal duration;
- stale temporary-exhibition details;
- generic “three reasons to visit”.

## Success criteria

The page communicates four collection areas, free permanent access and the museum’s own short/full route options without turning duration into an unsupported site recommendation.
