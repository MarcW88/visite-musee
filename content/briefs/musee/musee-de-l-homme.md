## Primary task

Explain the permanent Gallery of Man as an interdisciplinary inquiry into humanity and make the three official questions the page’s organising principle.

## Visit proposition

Choose the Musée de l’Homme when you want biology, prehistory, anthropology and social/cultural perspectives brought together around: who are we, where do we come from, where are we going?

## MUST

- preserve the official three-question structure;
- explain that the museum is broader than evolution/prehistory alone;
- current hours, ticket price and free conditions;
- museum-laboratory/research relationship where useful;
- no unsupported current-display claim for specific fossils/objects.

## SHOULD

- surface the free smartphone audioguide with 60+ scientist commentaries;
- clarify levels/route only if it improves orientation;
- connect collection examples to scientific questions rather than make a “top objects” list.

## Structure for this page

1. Decision answer + practical facts.
2. **Three questions** — retain three blocks because the number comes from the official gallery, not a template.
3. What kinds of evidence/collections answer those questions.
4. Research + audioguide/mediation as part of the visit.
5. Practical access.
6. Science/Paris/family next links.

## Anti-patterns

- removing the three-question structure merely to look different;
- treating the museum as only prehistoric fossils;
- list of “must-see objects” without display verification;
- invented duration or child-age recommendation.

## Success criteria

The visitor understands the museum’s inquiry model and can decide whether a question-led interdisciplinary visit fits their interests.
